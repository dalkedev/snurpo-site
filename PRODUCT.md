# Snurpo website

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Static, portable HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No backend or live capture in the marketing page. English at / and Polish at /pl/.

## Users
Developers diagnosing HTTP(S) traffic on a desktop, evaluating a local open-source debugger.

## Product Purpose
Give Snurpo an iconic home, demonstrate its real debugging workflow and lead visitors to the appropriate desktop download or source.

## Brand Commitments
Snurpo is an inquisitive mint creature with an enormous lavender nose, cream eyes and charcoal outlines. Preserve the supplied mascot and curious, deadpan voice. Non-generic, playful, memorable and interactive are explicit user requests. No em dash in copy or metadata. No cursor, macOS sharing strip or system titlebar in screenshots.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Snurpo 1.5.0 is a local desktop HTTP(S) debugger: capture, inspect, rules, mock, block, replay, Copy as cURL, saved SQLite sessions, analysis and optional MCP. AGPL-3.0-or-later. No account or subscription for local capture, sessions and export. Windows/Linux interactive validation remains open; macOS arm64 tested. No invented usage numbers, customer logos, speed claims, hosted service or automatic safety claims. cURL is POSIX; preview does not send a request. Android is experimental and secondary. No WebSocket frames, streaming or HTTP3. Demonstration traffic is illustrative and labeled concisely.

## Evidence
Local repository ../snurpo at release 1.5.0; https://github.com/dalkedev/snurpo . Clean native 1.4.2 gallery and 1.5.0 cURL screenshots with provenance. Mascot and banner in ../snurpo-brand.
